## What is Ledger?

Most Discord economy bots are casinos with a city skin. You `/work`, you `/daily`, you `/coinflip`, and nothing you do matters beyond the number in your balance.

Ledger is different.

It's a persistent simulation where every action is recorded, every record has consequences, and the world keeps moving whether you're online or not.

## The Systems

- **DBN (Discord Bad Number)** — Your permanent identity. Like an SSN. Jobs, records, bank accounts, and family ties are all attached to it. It never changes.
- **Bank & Cards** — Open an account, get a debit card. Your card number is your username, so anyone who has it can try to spend your money. A 6-digit PIN protects you.
- **Jobs & Clock** — Jobs run on an in-game clock. Apply, get hired, show up for shifts. Miss too many and you're fired.
- **Crime & Records** — Theft, hacking, fraud. Get caught and it goes on your permanent record. Records block jobs and escalate jail sentences.
- **Jail** — Served in in-game time. While locked up, your bank freezes and you might lose your job.
- **Lottery** — Weekly scratch tickets and a live drawing. Jackpot rolls over if nobody wins.
- **Family** — Adoption only. Build a family tree. Assets can be inherited.

## The Philosophy

Most economy bots are a list of commands. Ledger is a web of systems that talk to each other.

Your record affects your job. Your job affects your money. Your money makes you a target. Getting caught affects your record.

Every action is written down.

## Privacy & Trust

- **Slash commands only.** Ledger never reads your messages.
- **No privileged intents.** It doesn't request the full member list or message content.
- **Minimal permissions.** View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links. Nothing more.
- **Full transparency.** `/my data` shows you everything Ledger knows about you.
